Frequently Asked Questions for Employers

How quickly can our factory receive candidate inquiries through DigiGarment?

Many factory listings receive candidate responses within 12 to 24 hours of going live, depending on the role, location, and season. Because candidates reach out directly via phone or WhatsApp, HR managers can schedule practical trials immediately.

Is posting garment jobs on DigiGarment free for employers?

Yes. DigiGarment provides free job posting for garment manufacturers, textile processing units, and export companies in Tiruppur. There are no registration fees, no listing charges, and no platform hiring commission.

What types of garment roles can we recruit through this portal?

You can post for all garment manufacturing divisions, including production merchandisers, pattern masters (manual & CAD), cutting masters, tailor operators (Overlock, Flatlock, Single Needle), QA/QC checkers, machine mechanics, IE executives, and factory HR personnel.

How do we review candidate experience before conducting an on-site trial?

When candidates apply or contact you, you can review their reported factory background, machine skills, and previous departments. Employers can ask targeted technical questions over phone or WhatsApp prior to inviting the applicant for a practical floor trial.

Can buying houses and recruitment agencies post vacancies on DigiGarment?

Yes. Sourcing offices, liaison agencies, and authorized hiring consultants can register as employers to recruit merchandising, sampling, quality inspection, and compliance personnel in Tirupur.
