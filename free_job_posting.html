Frequently Asked Questions About Free Job Posting

Is DigiGarment free for employers to post jobs?

Yes. DigiGarment provides free job posting for garment manufacturers, textile processing houses, and export companies in Tiruppur. There are no registration fees, no listing charges, and no platform hiring commission.

How many job vacancies can our garment unit post?

Garment factories, export units, and job work contractors in Tiruppur can post vacancies across different departments (cutting, stitching, quality, merchandising) from their employer dashboard.

Does DigiGarment charge any commission when we hire a candidate?

No. DigiGarment does not charge placement fees, commissions, or referral fees upon successful candidate hiring. All hiring decisions and wage terms are arranged directly between your company and the candidate.

How quickly do candidates contact our factory after posting?

Many active job seekers browse TirupurGarment.in regularly. Depending on the department, required skills, and salary offered, employers typically receive candidate calls or WhatsApp messages within 12 to 24 hours of publishing.

Can I edit or remove the job posting once the vacancy is filled?

Yes. Through your employer dashboard, you can update job details at any time or close the listing as soon as your vacancy is filled to prevent further inquiries.
